Build a dataset of all photos with date, starred (favorited) status, and downloaded status (present on the local machine). Compare all vs. starred vs. downloaded to find gaps: days with many photos and zero local, or few stars / few downloads. Lives outside the wali repo: it targets a separate upstream issue and is specific to one library. Follow-up: if Google Takeout can be scripted or accepts a file list, use the gap set to sample photos and download them.
