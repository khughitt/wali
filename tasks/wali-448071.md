Favorited backgrounds (walictl favorites) should reach the phone as phone-ready images: scaled and cropped to the phone's screen (portrait aspect, native resolution), not the raw desktop originals.

Open questions to settle at scoping:
- Crop policy: center crop vs. a per-image focal point (the panel's edit recipes / variant cache from wali-608311 may already hold crop intent worth reusing).
- Target profile: where the phone's resolution/aspect lives (config.toml per-host section, or a separate device profile).
- Transport: Syncthing/Dropbox folder, adb push, KDE Connect/LocalSend, or rsync over Tailscale; whether the sync is one-way mirror (unfavorite removes from phone).
- Trigger: on favorite toggle, a walictl subcommand (e.g. `walictl phone sync`), or a systemd timer.
- Constraint: walictl stays stdlib-only Python; image work goes through the existing magick pipeline.
